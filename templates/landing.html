About TechAura

Techaura is a National-level technical festival organized by the final year students of the School of Engineering & Technology, Nagaland University. The festival creates an atmosphere of technological innovation, encouraging individuals to showcase their potential through competitions, presentations, exhibitions, and seminars.

More than just a celebration of technology and culture, Techaura serves as a platform to inspire students to become forward-thinking leaders and entrepreneurs. It challenges young minds to think beyond conventional boundaries and explore innovative ideas.

Over the years, the festival has featured diverse events including Voice of Techaura, Beat Contest, Painting Competition, Ramen Eating Competitions, Esports, and Mr & Miss Techaura - where the winner of Miss Techaura receives direct entry to the prestigious Nagaland Exotic Model Hunt by Blue Curves.

Why Choose TechAura?

Tech Showcase: Techaura provides an exciting platform for tech enthusiasts, showcasing innovative projects, hosting dynamic events, and delivering exhilarating concert at end of the event.

Skill Development: The festival features various competitions and events that challenge participants and help them develop their skills in areas like coding, robotics, and design.

Unity in Diversity: As a premier national-level technical festival hosted in Nagaland, Techaura unites students from diverse backgrounds to collaborate, innovate, and immerse themselves in a rich blend of cultural and technological experiences.
